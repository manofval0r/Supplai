# GBSN Social Logistics Challenge: Official Evidence Brief

**Research date:** 3 November 2026  
**Decision supported:** How Supplai should be scoped and presented to be a credible, high-scoring Health and Humanitarian Supply Chains entry.  
**Source standard:** First-party GBSN pages and GBSN-hosted previous-submission artefacts. This brief separates verified requirements from product inferences.

## Executive conclusion

Supplai fits the current **Health and Humanitarian Supply Chains** track directly: the track explicitly names essential-medicine stockouts, last-mile delivery to underserved clinics, and supply-chain resilience. The strongest interpretation of the evidence is not “build the most features.” It is: prove one local, human-operated logistics loop with a digital layer that makes it safer, faster, and measurable.

For Supplai, that loop should be: **a named PHC reports a defined essential-medicine exception -> an authorised source and LGA workflow verify an eligible transfer -> a rider collects and delivers it -> both sides record proof -> an exception/impact record closes.** A facility app and operations web interface should be the primary visible experience. SMS or WhatsApp should be an accessible intake and notification channel into that same canonical order, not the centre of the product.

The official creative-illustration rules expressly permit a **digital prototype or website**, and set an approximately three-minute duration or non-video equivalent. A polished, working PWA/web demonstration plus a concise motion screen-recording is therefore an appropriate illustration format. It does not replace the written report, feasibility model, and local evidence.

### Current AI-policy conflict: resolve before submission work

The current official page says entries should be completed independently and that teams **should not use generative AI** to develop, write, analyse or substantially modify submissions; it also says GBSN may ask how work was developed and may consider inappropriate or undisclosed AI use in determining eligibility. [GBSN challenge page](https://gbsn.org/slc/)

This conflicts with the team’s current understanding that AI is allowed. That understanding may come from a private clarification or a different version of the rules, but no such written ruling was reviewed in this research. **Priority action: obtain and preserve an explicit written clarification from `programs@gbsn.org` before any AI-assisted output is used in a submitted report, illustration, script, or analysis.** Until then, treat this research and any AI-produced plan as internal planning only, not submission material.

## What the published criteria signal

The current 2026 page describes the deliverables and two-phase process but does **not** publish scoring weights. An archived official GBSN challenge page, useful only as historical context, says preliminary judges assessed: problem description/local relevance, creativity, feasibility, SDG contribution, and the intersection of people and technology. It also required a logistics-based social solution, societal benefit, local relevance, multidisciplinary work, SDG alignment, and human plus digital elements. [Archived GBSN criteria](https://gbsn.org/conference/slc-old/)

**Inference, not a current rubric:** Supplai should make every one of those dimensions visible, but should not state that it knows the 2026 scoring weights.

### Important limitation about prior winners

GBSN’s summaries describe **proposals** and solution packages. They do not prove that previous winners deployed production software or that a live app is required to win. The evidence supports showing a credible operating model with a strong visual illustration, not claiming that a functional app alone is the judging standard.

## Product implications for Supplai

These are reasoned recommendations, not official requirements:

1. **Make the product an LGA-authorised emergency redistribution control tower.** It should complement existing procurement and LMIS processes by managing the exception between stockout recognition and approved resupply.
2. **Keep the app/web interface primary.** Demonstrate three role-specific surfaces: facility requester, LGA operations officer and delivery rider. This makes the people-and-technology intersection visible.
3. **Make SMS and WhatsApp an adapter, not the primary workflow.** A message creates the same request record seen in the app/web; later app sessions show its status. Use a transparent simulator or adapter boundary when provider accounts are unavailable. Do not present a simulated channel as live.
4. **Show the full control loop, not merely ordering.** The visual narrative should include eligibility/safety-stock rules, source acceptance, authorisation, rider handoff, live transfer state, delivery proof, exception escalation and impact metrics.
5. **Show only a defensible scope.** A named LGA, 6-8 fictional/demo PHCs, two approved ambient-stable tracer commodities, one operating window and one carrier arrangement are easier to defend than a national marketplace.
6. **Use one high-clarity three-minute proof sequence.** Start with a clinical stockout, switch to the LGA exception board, show source approval and rider tracking, end with verified receipt and the measurable outcome. The app should have polished motion, but the narration must explain the logistics mechanism and operating ownership.
7. **Prepare the unanswered questions.** The report and final Q&A should state the largest assumption, the failure condition, the buyer/payer, partner roles, data governance, quality/traceability controls, escalation when no eligible source exists, and how impact will be measured.

## Research limitations and follow-ups

- GBSN has not published a numeric 2026 scoring rubric on the current public page. The archived dimensions are directional only.
- The current page provides a date but not a time zone, portal upload details or any verified exception to the AI policy. Confirm these directly with GBSN.
- Official winner summaries may be promotional and are insufficient for comparing feature depth. They are used here only to identify themes GBSN itself highlighted.
- No local LGA, PHC, commodity, legal or provider validation was carried out in this research. Those need human-led field evidence before being claimed in the entry.
